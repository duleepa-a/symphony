## Main Track: Hack the Future with Jac

### Judging Criteria
1. **Technical Execution:** Excellent use of Jac and Jaseci.
2. **Depth of Agentic Behavior**
3. **Creativity & Innovation:** Impact and novelty.
4. **Presentation:** Clear presentation and demo video.

---

## Submission Checklist (Due 5:30 PM)

* **GitHub Link:** Codebase must be at least 80% Jac.
* **Demo Video:** A short video showcasing the working project.
* **Written Description:** Summary including details on how Jac or Jaseci tools were utilized.
* **Star the Repo:** Star the Jac repository on GitHub (`github.com/jaseci-labs/jac`).
* **Encouraged:** Host the project on Jac Hammer (free sandbox) for the judges.

---

## Rules & Prize Eligibility

1. Every team member must register and check in on the day of the event.
2. Demo video must be submitted by 5:30 PM.
3. All coding must take place during official hacking hours. (Brainstorming beforehand is allowed; repositories will be checked).
4. Exactly **3 people per team**.
5. One project per team.
6. Submissions must be submitted on time via the provided link; late entries will be ineligible to win.
7. Must present working software that meaningfully uses Jac (**80% or more**).
8. Be kind and build in good faith. Misrepresenting a project will result in disqualification.
